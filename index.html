FAQ

What does the free preview include?

Exactly what each feature page says — real output, watermarked where it includes images. If the preview doesn't impress you, don't pay. That's the whole point.

Is this really AI doing the work?

Yes. Large language models plus image models on Cloudflare, with structured recipes per feature. That's why it's $49 and minutes instead of $4,900 and 4 weeks. We don't pretend a designer touched it — the recipes are the product.

What exactly do I get for $49?

The full deliverable for that feature — e.g. a 10-point annotated homepage teardown PDF, or 5 ad creatives in 2 ratios with 3 headline variants each. Every feature page lists exactly what's included.

Do you keep my logo / screenshots / copy?

We keep your inputs long enough to produce the deliverable, then they're deleted. We never sell data, never train on it, never use your brand in marketing without permission.

What if the deliverable is bad?

Who owns the output?

You do. Full commercial rights, forever. Use it in ads, on your site, wherever.

No. Every feature is a one-time $49. Bundles are one-time too. If we add features later, you buy only the ones you want.
